# Editing the MGIRL website — a guide for admins

You don't need to know any code. Everything below happens in a simple web form.

## Signing in

1. Go to **`https://<the-site-address>/admin/`**.
2. Type the **shared admin code** and click **Sign in**.
3. You'll see the content dashboard.

## What you can do

On the left you'll see: **People, Publications, Projects, News & Announcements,
Events.** Click any of them.

- **Add** something new → click **"New …"** (top right), fill the form, click
  **Publish**.
- **Edit** something → click it in the list, change the fields, **Publish**.
- **Delete** → open the item → **Delete** (top right).
- **Reorder people** → use the **Order** number on each person (smaller = higher).
- **Upload a photo** → click the image field and choose a file from your computer.

The website updates itself a minute or two after you publish.

## The handy bits

- **Pin an announcement to the homepage:** in a News item, switch **"Pin to top
  of homepage"** on.
- **Link a paper to its authors:** when adding a Publication, the **"Lab
  authors"** field lets you pick lab members from a dropdown. The paper then
  shows up on each of their profile pages automatically — you only enter the
  paper once, even if several lab members are authors.
- **Link to the real journal:** use the **"Link to authentic journal page"** and
  **DOI** fields so visitors can reach the published article.
- **Feature something on the homepage:** most items have a **"Feature on
  homepage"** switch.

## If something looks wrong

- Changes not showing? Wait ~2 minutes (the site rebuilds), then refresh.
- Forgot the code, or need to change who can edit? Contact the site maintainer.

That's it — add, edit, publish. You can't break the page design from here, so
feel free to explore.
