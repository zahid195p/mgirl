Dr. Muhammad Umair Sial leads the Molecular Genetics of Insecticide Resistance
Laboratory (MGIRL) at the Department of Entomology, University of Agriculture
Faisalabad. His research focuses on understanding how insects develop resistance
to insecticides at the molecular level, and on developing practical,
sustainable resistance-management strategies.
